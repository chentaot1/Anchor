# Windows 1.0.7

Meet the Harbor Pilot: the AI assistant is renamed and recolored as the green starboard light that guides you into harbor. "AI Tools" becomes "Pilot" in the sidebar with a "HARBOR PILOT" header, and every AI accent moves from violet to luminous pilot-teal — chosen to complete a true split-complementary scheme with the beacon orange, and to belong to the harbor world (shallow water, sea-glass, channel lights) instead of generic AI purple.

The whole app goes premium-feeling: Nunito display type paired with Atkinson Hyperlegible body text, a glowing NOW beacon card, softly staggered deck entrances, warm beacon glow on primary actions, and glass-like top sheens on dark surfaces. Dusk skies glint ember and the aurora burns green-teal. Motion throughout respects the system reduced-motion setting.

The MSI and EXE installers include Microsoft OpenJDK 17, its unchanged legal directory and Access Bridge. Installers remain unsigned. The separate model weights and `llama-server` are user-installed and retain their upstream licenses.

Shared, desktop and Android unit tests passed and Android lint is clean. Physical screen-reader interaction was not repeated for this release.
